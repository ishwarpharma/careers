About Ishwar Pharma

Maharashtra's Most Trusted Pharma Wholesale Partner

Since 2001, Ishwar Pharma has been a leading wholesaler and super distributor of generic medicines across Maharashtra. We are C&F and Super Distributors for all companies we deal in — purchasing directly from manufacturers, often with sole distribution rights for entire Maharashtra. From Mumbai Dawa Bazar to the most remote districts, we ensure timely delivery of essential pharmaceuticals to chemists, hospitals, exporters, and wholesalers.
